Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects only what it needs to run your expense tracker:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense entries — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you add.
  • Basic technical data — such as session cookies needed to keep you signed in.

We do not connect to your bank accounts and never ask for card numbers or banking credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the service to you, including:

  • Signing you in and keeping your account secure.
  • Storing and displaying your expenses.
  • Generating category breakdowns and spending summaries.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and protected with reasonable security measures. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your expense data is removed as well.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic request information such as your IP address when your browser fetches them.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data deleted, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
